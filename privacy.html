Privacy Policy

MyBrainBubble

Last updated: 15 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyBrainBubble (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the MyBrainBubble mobile application (the “App”). In this Policy, “user”, “you”, or “your” refers to any individual who accesses or uses the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

MyBrainBubble is a wellbeing, reflection, and organisation app designed to help users journal, track habits, organise tasks, and reflect on their lives.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, MyBrainBubble acts as the data controller for personal data processed through the App.

MyBrainBubble processes personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Where required by law, MyBrainBubble is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) as a data controller.

Contact:

brainbubbletech@outlook.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate and maintain the App.

A. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • email address
  • username
  • subscription tier
  • device identifiers
  • account creation timestamps
  • login timestamps

This information is used to create and maintain your account.

MyBrainBubble does not actively monitor or read private journal entries. Journal entries remain private to the account holder unless the user chooses to share them with another user.

B. Content You Create

You may create or upload content within the App, including:

  • journal entries
  • notes and reflections
  • photos or videos attached to entries
  • habit logs
  • reminders
  • mood logs
  • sleep logs
  • water intake logs
  • menstrual cycle logs
  • financial tracking entries
  • task lists
  • personal templates
  • shared journal entries

You retain ownership of the content you create.

Some content, such as private journal entries, may be encrypted on your device before being uploaded to our servers. Where encryption is applied, MyBrainBubble does not have access to the readable contents of that data.

Other types of content, including logs, templates, calendar data, and organisational features, are stored in a readable format to allow the App to provide functionality such as syncing, insights, and structured views.

Some content may contain personal or sensitive information such as emotional reflections, health-related notes, or personal experiences. This information is entered voluntarily by users and is processed only to provide the App’s functionality.

We follow the principle of data minimisation and only collect the personal data necessary to provide and maintain the App’s features.

If you choose to share a journal entry with another user, a version of that content may be processed and stored in a format that allows it to be accessed by the recipient.

C. AI Chat Messages

If you use the AI chat feature (“Vent Bubble”), the messages you send within that feature may be processed by an AI service provider to generate responses.

Important:

  • Journal entries are not automatically analysed by the AI system.
  • The AI system only processes messages sent directly within the AI chat interface.

AI responses are generated automatically and may not always be accurate.

MyBrainBubble does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

D. Media Uploads

Users may upload photos or videos within certain parts of the App, such as journal entries or templates.

These media files may be stored as part of the user’s account data in order to provide the App’s features.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload any media content they provide.

E. Subscription & Payment Information

All payments are processed through:

  • Apple App Store (iOS devices)
  • Google Play Store (Android devices)

We do not receive or store full payment card details.

We may receive limited information from the platform providers such as:

  • subscription status
  • billing period
  • renewal status
  • cancellation status

This information is used only to manage subscription access.

F. Technical and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • device type
  • operating system version
  • app version
  • crash logs
  • basic diagnostic data

This information helps us maintain stability and improve the App.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data only where necessary to operate and improve the App.

This includes:

  • creating and managing user accounts
  • providing journaling and tracking features
  • enabling sharing features
  • generating AI chat responses when requested
  • managing subscriptions
  • sending optional notifications
  • improving performance and reliability
  • preventing misuse or violations of our Terms

We do not sell personal data.

We do not use private journal content for advertising purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

Contract

To provide the App’s services to users.

Legitimate Interests

To improve functionality, security, and reliability.

Consent

For optional features such as notifications or analytics.

Legal Obligation

Where required to comply with applicable laws.

5. AI and Automated Features

Certain features within the App may generate automated insights or responses.

These features:

  • operate automatically
  • generate informational reflections
  • do not make decisions with legal or significant personal effects

AI responses are not a substitute for professional advice.

The AI system processes only the content submitted directly within the AI chat interface.

Journal entries and personal logs are not automatically processed by the AI system.

6. Sharing and Community Features

The App may allow users to share journal entries with selected users.

When you share content:

  • the recipient may view that content within the App
  • archived entries may remain visible to recipients
  • Deleting an entry removes access within the App

Once another user has accessed shared content, we cannot control how that user stores or shares it outside the App.

7. Data Storage and Security

User data is stored using a secure cloud infrastructure.

Different types of data are stored in different ways depending on their purpose.

Encryption is applied selectively to certain types of data and may not apply to all features within the App.

Private journal entries may be encrypted on your device before being transmitted and stored, meaning MyBrainBubble does not have access to the readable contents of that data.

Other data, including templates, logs, calendar data, Brain Fog entries, and similar organisational features, are stored in a readable format to allow the App to function correctly across devices and provide insights.

AI chat messages are processed to generate responses and may be transmitted to third-party providers for this purpose.

MyBrainBubble uses Supabase as its backend infrastructure for:

  • database storage
  • authentication
  • file storage

Security measures include:

  • encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • authentication controls
  • infrastructure security measures

Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using secure industry-standard protocols (such as HTTPS/TLS).

While we take reasonable steps to protect data, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Access to user data is restricted and limited to what is necessary to operate and maintain the App.

Due to the use of encryption for certain types of data, MyBrainBubble may not be able to recover encrypted content if access to a user’s account or device is lost.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the App and comply with legal obligations.

Examples include:

Account Data

Stored while your account remains active.

User Content (journals, logs, templates)

Stored until deleted by the user.

Shared Journal Entries

Stored until the entry is deleted or the account is removed.

Free Mode Preview Data

Data created in free preview mode may be automatically deleted after approximately 24 hours.

Technical Logs

May be retained temporarily for troubleshooting.

If you delete your account, your data will be deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law.
