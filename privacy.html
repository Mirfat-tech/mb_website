Privacy Policy

MyBrainBubble

Last updated: 3 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyBrainBubble (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the MyBrainBubble mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

MyBrainBubble is a wellbeing, reflection, and organisation app designed to help users:

  • organise thoughts
  • track habits
  • plan daily life
  • reflect using structured tools

For the purposes of UK data protection law, MyBrainBubble acts as the data controller.

We comply with:

  • UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
  • Data Protection Act 2018

Contact: brainbubbletech@outlook.com

2. Who Can Use the App

MyBrainBubble is rated suitable for users aged 4 and above.

If you are under 18, or the age of majority in your country, you should use the App with guidance from a parent or legal guardian where appropriate.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is necessary to provide the App.

3. What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data required to operate the App.

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • email address
  • username
  • subscription status
  • device identifiers, for device limits
  • login timestamps

We do not have access to your password.

B. Content You Create (Stored Locally)

You may create content such as:

  • journal entries
  • Brain Fog entries
  • Gratitude Bubble entries
  • Quote Bubble entries
  • planner data
  • habit tracking
  • templates and logs
  • reminders and notes
  • photos or videos
  • visual customisation, such as user-added images
  • custom themes, including colours and visual preferences
  • user-created templates

Important: Local Storage Model

MyBrainBubble is designed as a local-first application.

This means:

  • Your content is stored only on your device
  • We do not store your personal content on our servers
  • We do not access, read, or monitor your content

This includes:

  • journals
  • planner data
  • habits
  • Brain Fog entries
  • Gratitude Bubble entries
  • Quote Bubble entries
  • templates and logs
  • Pomodoro usage
  • uploaded media

C. Limited External Data (Account Only)

The only data stored externally is limited to:

  • your email address
  • basic account details
  • number of devices linked to your account, for subscription purposes

This is used strictly for:

  • authentication
  • account management
  • subscription enforcement

D. Media Content

If you add photos or videos:

  • they are stored locally on your device
  • we do not access or store them externally

You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to use any uploaded content.

E. Subscription Information

Subscriptions are handled by:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play Store

We may receive:

  • subscription status
  • billing state

We do not receive or store payment card details.

F. Technical Data

We may collect limited technical data such as:

  • device type
  • operating system
  • app version
  • crash logs

This helps us maintain and improve the App.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use data only to:

  • provide core App functionality
  • manage your account
  • enforce subscription limits, such as device count
  • improve performance and reliability
  • detect and prevent misuse

We do not:

  • sell your data
  • use your data for advertising
  • access or analyse your personal content

5. Data Sharing

We only share data where necessary:

Service Providers

We use trusted providers such as:

  • authentication and infrastructure services
  • Apple / Google, for payments

They process only the limited data required to provide their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate security measures, including:

  • encrypted data transfer (HTTPS/TLS)
  • secure authentication systems

However:

  • no system is completely secure
  • data stored on your device is your responsibility

7. Data Retention

We retain only limited account-related data:

  • Account data: while your account is active
  • Technical logs: temporary

Since most content is stored locally:

  • Deleting the App or your device data may permanently remove your content
  • We do not recover lost local data

Because your content is stored locally, you are responsible for maintaining backups of important data. We cannot recover lost content.

8. International Transfers

Some limited account data may be processed outside the UK. Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, are used.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you may:

  • access your data
  • correct your data
  • request deletion
  • restrict processing
  • object to processing
  • request data portability

Contact: brainbubbletech@outlook.com

You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for users aged 4+.

Where appropriate:

  • Parental or guardian guidance is recommended
  • If you believe data has been provided improperly, please contact us

11. Notifications

If enabled, the App may send:

  • reminders
  • planner alerts
  • habit notifications
  • timer alerts

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If significant changes are made:

  • We may notify you within the App
  • Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
