What MyBrainBubble actually is

MyBrainBubble is almost like your second brain. Or your brain... finally put into an app.

It’s a gentle wellbeing + life organisation app that helps you:

  • unload your thoughts
  • remember the important (and random) stuff
  • build habits without pressure
  • and slowly get your life together — in your own way

You can vent, journal, track your mood, water, sleep, money, habits, and even log movies, places, and memories — all connected to a calendar that shows how your days actually went.

No productivity guilt. No “fix yourself” energy. Just somewhere safe to put everything that lives in your head.

MyBrainBubble is for you if...

🫧 You want to text someone at 3am ...but don’t want to wake your friends, partner, ex, or situationship. So you vent to somewhere that listens back.
🫧 You forget to drink water ...and then suddenly remember at 11pm and feel personally attacked by your dry lips.
🫧 You want to get out of the rat race and “get your life together” — but you don’t feel organised, motivated, or even sure where to start.
🫧 Your brain holds everything at once — appointments, emotions, habits, goals, random thoughts — and you’re tired of carrying it all in your head.
🫧 You randomly panic about your passport at 3am even though you’re supposed to be sleeping and now you have to check where it is.
🫧 You’ve tried planners, journals, and productivity apps but they felt overwhelming, rigid, or made you feel like you were failing.
🫧 You want gentle reminders to drink water, check in with yourself, or do the thing — without being nagged or shamed.
